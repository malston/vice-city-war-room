# Kickoff post

Post this in the team channel to start a round. It is written in Slack formatting (`*bold*`, no `**`, no `#` headings). The owner approves the final text before it goes out.

```bash
*Vice City heist crew: casing the joint*

GTA 6 launches November 19, 2026. Time to plan the job. Copy the lines below, fill in what you want to share, and the crew writes your personal launch plan.

Console is PS5, Xbox Series X|S, or something else. PTO is approved, requested, or not yet for Nov 19-20. Spouse risk runs from 1 (fully on board) to 5 (send help). Preordered is yes or no. Weakness is the thing most likely to wreck your launch day.

*Console:*
*PTO:*
*Spouse risk:*
*Preordered:*
*Weakness:*

Partial answers are fine, and you can skip anything you would rather keep to yourself. Only people who reply get a plan. A second reply updates the first.

When the plans go up, react to the one you like best.
```

The intake fields match the profile contract in `contracts.md`: `console`, `pto_status`, `spouse_risk`, `preordered`, `weakness`.
