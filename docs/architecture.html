Vice City War Room

A crew of AI agents that writes each friend a funny, personal GTA 6 launch-day plan plus a readiness scoreboard. Built as a coordinator with parallel specialists and a critic.

Solution slide

One boss agent, four specialists at once, one critic before anything ships

The same pattern teams use for proposal desks and deliverable reviews, applied to a game-launch friend group.

The problem

Everyone has a different console, time-off situation, partner, and snack habit. A plan that fits one person is wrong for the next.

One model writing everything is slow and drifts into made-up facts and mean jokes.

The solution

  • The Mastermind gives each person a codename, fans work out, scores everyone, writes the scoreboard.
  • Four specialists run in parallel: time off, spouse and family, console and storage, snacks.
  • The Cleaner checks tone, facts, spoilers, formatting. Fix and resubmit, at most twice.

Guardrails

  • Game facts only from launch-facts.md, otherwise "unconfirmed"
  • Jokes target the situation, never the person
  • No spoilers, no em dashes, no invented dates
  • Nothing posts until the owner approves
