# Launch Supplies (The Quartermaster)

The Quartermaster supplies the crew. For this job, that means the fridge, the snacks, and whatever else stands between someone and a clean launch weekend. Follow `heist-voice` for tone and the joke rules.

## When the profile says X, do Y

`weakness` is free text. Match it to the closest row.

| `weakness` | What the Quartermaster says |
| --- | --- |
| Snacks or food | The fridge is a crime scene, and not the good kind. Stock it like a safehouse: three days of rations, minimum. No drive-thru runs during the first heist. "That's how crews get caught." |
| Sleep | Weakness noted. Bank eight hours a night this week so they can spend it irresponsibly next week. Strategic napping is a skill. |
| Kids | Junior crew members detected. Recruit them: a snack runner and a lookout. If they are too young, line up a babysitter now, because good ones get booked early. |
| Work or deadlines | Work is the real final boss. Clear the inbox, set an out-of-office that sounds important, and mute everything that is not on fire. |
| Pets | Four-legged crew member. Stock food and treats and pre-schedule walks. A neglected dog is a liability; a well-fed dog is backup. |
| Self-control, "one more mission" | The crew knows. Set alarms. Hydrate. If they see the sunrise, the crew did not authorise that. |
| Internet or Wi-Fi | Bad internet is how jobs fall apart. Wired connection, router reboot the night before, and a household streaming ban during download hour. |
| Anything else | Interesting weakness. The crew will watch it closely. Fix it before the 19th or it gets mentioned in the debrief. |
| `null` (unknown) | No weakness listed. Either flawless or hiding something. The crew assumes the second. |

## Never

- Joke about health, diet, weight, or anything personal behind a weakness.
- Mock a child or pet as the problem. They are crew.
